Who We Are

We Are WHI —
On a Mission

Born in Enugu, Nigeria with a global vision — we exist to turn the tide on nylon and plastic waste pollution, one community at a time.

Our Story

How It All Began

We looked around and saw a crisis hiding in plain sight — nylon bags in gutters, plastic bottles in rivers, waste choking communities that deserved better.

We asked ourselves: What if we stopped waiting for someone else to fix this?

And so WHI was born — with one belief: Pollution is not the end of the story. It is the beginning of possibility.

🌍 Our Vision

"Building a sustainable, waste-free world that is clean and safe for all generations, in alignment with the global green initiative."

🎯 Our Mission

"To partner with governments, organizations, schools and communities in waste management; to educate society on sanitation; and to empower youths through the waste-to-wealth initiative for a better tomorrow."

What We Do

Programs That
Create Real Change

Six programs designed to create lasting environmental change — meeting communities where they are and young people where they dream.

📢
Community Education & Awareness

We take waste management education directly into communities through workshops, seminars and outreach. When communities understand the problem, they become the solution.

🏫
School Engagement Program

We bring environmental education into classrooms — making it interactive and inspiring for students of all ages. Today's students are tomorrow's environmental champions.

♻️
Waste-to-Wealth Initiative

We train young people to convert nylon and plastic waste into sellable products — turning environmental pollution into economic empowerment and real jobs.

🧹
Clean Community Campaign

Our hands-on clean-up exercises physically remove nylon and plastic waste from communities and waterways — inspiring pride and building unity.

🏛️
Government & Institutional Partnership

We engage governments and institutions to advocate for stronger waste management policies and collaborate on community-level environmental projects.

🌿
Green Ambassador Program

We train passionate young people as environmental advocates in their communities — giving them the platform, tools and confidence to lead and inspire peers.
