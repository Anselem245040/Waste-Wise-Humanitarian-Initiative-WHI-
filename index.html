Who We Are

We Are WHI —
On a Mission

Born in Enugu, Nigeria with a global vision — we exist to turn the tide on nylon and plastic waste pollution, one community at a time.

Our Story

How It All Began

We looked around and saw a crisis hiding in plain sight — nylon bags in gutters, plastic bottles in rivers, waste choking communities that deserved better.

We asked ourselves: What if we stopped waiting for someone else to fix this?

And so WHI was born — with one belief: Pollution is not the end of the story. It is the beginning of possibility.

Our Leadership

Meet Our Founder

Chimdinma Zik is the Founder & CEO of Waste Wise Humanitarian Initiative — a humanitarian and environmental organization born from a simple question: What if we stopped waiting for someone else to fix this?

Under her leadership, WHI is tackling nylon and plastic pollution at its root — educating communities, empowering youth through waste-to-wealth initiatives, and transforming pollution into possibility.

She believes that a sustainable nylon and plastic waste-free world begins when waste is separated at its source — and she is building WHI to make that vision a reality, one community at a time.

Our Model

WHI Source Separation Model

We use our WHI Source Separation Model (WSSM) to address nylon and plastic waste pollution from the grassroots. The model views households, schools, communities, businesses, and industries as the first line of defense against nylon and plastic pollution.

Our Vision

"Building a sustainable, waste-free world that is clean and safe for all generations, in alignment with the global green initiative."

Our Mission

"To partner with governments, organizations, schools and communities in waste management; to educate society on the importance of sanitation; and to empower youths through the waste-to-wealth initiative for a better tomorrow."

Our Objectives

What We Aim to Achieve

  1. 1 To raise awareness on the dangers of nylon and plastic waste pollution globally.
  2. 2 To educate communities, schools and organizations on proper waste management and improved sanitation practices.
  3. 3 To partner with governments and institutions in developing and implementing waste management policies.
  4. 4 To empower youth through waste-to-wealth initiatives that generate economic opportunities.
  5. 5 To promote a culture of environmental responsibility across all demographics.
  6. 6 To contribute to the United Nations SDGs, particularly SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities) and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

What We Do

Programs That
Create Real Change

Six programs designed to create lasting environmental change — meeting communities where they are and young people where they dream.

📢
Community Education & Awareness

We take waste management education directly into communities through workshops, seminars and outreach. When communities understand the problem, they become the solution.

🏫
School Engagement Program

We bring environmental education into classrooms — making it interactive and inspiring for students of all ages. Today's students are tomorrow's environmental champions.

♻️
Waste-to-Wealth Initiative

We train young people to convert nylon and plastic waste into sellable products — turning environmental pollution into economic empowerment and real jobs.

🧹
Clean Community Campaign

Our hands-on clean-up exercises physically remove nylon and plastic waste from communities and waterways — inspiring pride and building unity.

🏛️
Government & Institutional Partnership

We engage governments and institutions to advocate for stronger waste management policies and collaborate on community-level environmental projects.

🌿
Green Ambassador Program

We train passionate young people as environmental advocates in their communities — giving them the platform, tools and confidence to lead and inspire peers.
